**YTTranscript and youtube-transcript.io both turn a YouTube URL into text in seconds. The real difference is how you pay.** youtube-transcript.io is a subscription built for volume: 25 free tokens a month, then $9.99 to $79.99 a month for higher volume, bulk extraction and extended API access. YTTranscript is pay-as-you-go: **2 free transcripts a day with no signup**, one-time packs from **$9 for 100 transcripts** whose credits never expire, and AI transcription for videos that have no captions. Pick youtube-transcript.io for steady high volume or automation. Pick YTTranscript for light or unpredictable use, caption-less videos, or if you'd rather not add another subscription.

Not to be confused with youtube-transcript.ai, a separate tool we cover in [YTTranscript vs youtube-transcript.ai](/yttranscript-vs-youtube-transcript-ai).

## Where youtube-transcript.io wins

- **Bulk extraction.** Paid plans add playlist and CSV bulk jobs, so a 300-video playlist is one job instead of 300 pastes. For the manual route, see [how to download YouTube playlist transcripts](/how-to-download-youtube-playlist-transcripts).
- **Developer and automation access.** Its API accepts up to **50 video IDs per request** (rate-limited to 5 requests per 10 seconds), Plus and Pro add a Channels API, and there are official **n8n and Make** templates. YTTranscript doesn't advertise a public API; if you need code, start with our [YouTube transcript API guide](/youtube-transcript-api).
- **Cost at steady scale.** At 1,000+ transcripts a month, roughly 0.8–1¢ per transcript beats YTTranscript's best pack rate of 1.5¢.
- **Power-user extras.** AI summaries, plus chat with a transcript, watchlists and 90-day history on Plus. Pro keeps history indefinitely. VTT and CSV exports suit subtitle and spreadsheet workflows.

## Accuracy: a tie on captioned videos

For any video that already has captions, both tools return the same text, because both read YouTube's caption track rather than re-transcribing the audio. Quality depends on whether the creator uploaded captions or YouTube generated them automatically; see [auto-generated vs manual transcripts](/youtube-auto-generated-vs-manual-transcript). The gap only appears on caption-less videos, where youtube-transcript.io returns nothing and YTTranscript's AI transcription fills in.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is youtube-transcript.io free?**
Partly. Its free plan includes 25 tokens a month, and one transcript uses one token. Beyond that you need Plus at $9.99/month for 1,000 transcripts or Pro at $24.99/month for 3,000, and the monthly allowance resets each billing cycle.

**What is the main difference between YTTranscript and youtube-transcript.io?**
The pricing model. youtube-transcript.io is a monthly subscription built for volume, with bulk playlist extraction and an API. YTTranscript gives 2 free transcripts a day with no signup and sells one-time packs whose credits never expire.

**Which is cheaper, YTTranscript or youtube-transcript.io?**
For steady volume of 1,000 or more transcripts a month, youtube-transcript.io is cheaper at roughly 0.8 to 1 cent per transcript. For light or irregular use, YTTranscript usually costs less: the free tier covers 2 videos a day and packs start at $9 for 100 transcripts that never expire.

**Can youtube-transcript.io transcribe videos without captions?**
No. Its own policy says it only fetches captions that already exist on YouTube, and it does not support private, unlisted, age-restricted or geoblocked videos. YTTranscript can AI-generate a transcript for a caption-less video, which uses about 10 credits.

**Which tool is better for bulk playlist or channel transcripts?**
youtube-transcript.io. Its paid plans add bulk playlist and CSV extraction and a Channels API, and its API accepts 50 video IDs per request. YTTranscript works one video at a time and does not advertise batch extraction.
