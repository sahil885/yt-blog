Almost every "free YouTube transcriber" has a catch: a monthly cap, a required account, a Chrome extension, or no download button. We compared the free tiers only, on what you get before you pay:

- **Free limit:** how many transcripts before you hit a wall
- **Account:** whether you must sign up first
- **Extension:** whether you must install anything
- **Output:** copy only, or real files
- **Mobile:** whether it works on a phone

## 2. YouTube's Show transcript: best free option for one-off reading

Open a video, expand the description and click **Show transcript**. It's free with no limit and no account. The catch: there's no download button, copying from the panel is fiddly, and on the mobile app you can read it but not easily copy it. Good for skimming one video; tedious for saving text.

## 3. DownSub: best free subtitle file downloader

DownSub is a free web app: paste a URL and download the captions as **SRT, TXT or VTT**, no signup and no extension. It's built for subtitle files rather than readable text, so a TXT download can still read like caption fragments. Pick it if you need an SRT for a video editor; for clean paragraphs, a transcript tool reads better ([downloading YouTube subtitles](/download-youtube-subtitles)).

## 4. youtube-transcript.io: most free transcripts per month

The free plan gives **25 transcripts a month** with a 3-day history, and the site says you can start without signing up. Paid plans start at **$9.99/month for 1,000** (Pro is $24.99/month for 3,000). It's the most generous monthly free quota here, and a good fit if you transcribe a few videos a week.

## 5. Tactiq: free if you also transcribe meetings

Tactiq is mainly a meeting transcriber for Google Meet, Zoom and Teams, with a free YouTube transcript generator on the side. The account plan includes **10 transcripts and 5 AI credits a month**; Pro is **$8/user/month billed annually**. Worth it if meetings are your main need.

## 6. NoteGPT: free transcripts with AI notes

NoteGPT bundles transcripts with AI summaries, notes and mind maps. Each account gets **15 free quotas a month**, spent across features, so heavy AI use burns through them quickly. Pro is **$9.99/month** ($9/month billed annually). Good for students who want notes in the same app.

## 7. Whisper: best free option for videos with no captions

Every tool above reads YouTube's existing captions. If a video has none, OpenAI's open-source **Whisper** transcribes the audio itself, for free, on your own computer. You need to install it and download the audio, and long videos take time without a good GPU. See [what to do when a YouTube video has no transcript](/youtube-video-no-transcript).
