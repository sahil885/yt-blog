## Prompt Templates for Different Summary Types

### Executive summary (for business content)
```
Summarize this YouTube transcript as a 1-paragraph executive summary suitable for sharing with a team. Focus on decisions, recommendations, and key data points. Omit personal anecdotes.

[PASTE TRANSCRIPT]
```

### Key takeaways (for educational content)
```
Here is a lecture or tutorial transcript. List the 5 most important things to understand or remember from this video. Use plain language.

[PASTE TRANSCRIPT]
```

### Action items (for how-to or tutorial videos)
```
This is a transcript of a tutorial video. Extract a numbered step-by-step action list of everything I need to do to apply what was taught. Be specific and sequential.

[PASTE TRANSCRIPT]
```

### TL;DR (for long interviews or podcasts)
```
Summarize this transcript in 3 sentences or fewer. Assume the reader has no context. Just the most important point.

[PASTE TRANSCRIPT]
```

## How Long Does It Take?

The entire workflow — from video URL to finished summary — takes about 60-90 seconds: copying the URL (5 seconds), getting the transcript at YTTranscript (5-10 seconds), copying the transcript (2 seconds), pasting into ChatGPT with a prompt (10 seconds), and waiting for the AI response (10-20 seconds).

## What About Long Videos?

A 1-hour video produces roughly 8,000-12,000 words of transcript. ChatGPT's free tier handles transcripts up to about 10,000-15,000 words. For very long videos, use Claude (claude.ai) which has a larger context window, or summarize in parts.

## Tips for Better Summaries

**Be specific about what you want.** "Summarize this" gets a generic result. "Summarize this in 5 bullet points focused on the business advice given, ignoring personal anecdotes" gets great results.

**Ask follow-up questions.** After getting the summary, keep the conversation going — the AI still has the full transcript in context.
