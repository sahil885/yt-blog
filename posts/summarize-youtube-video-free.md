## Prompt Templates for Different Summary Types

The basic summary prompt works well, but you can tailor the output to your exact need:

### Executive summary (for business content)
```
Summarize this YouTube transcript as a 1-paragraph executive summary suitable for sharing with a team. Focus on decisions, recommendations, and key data points. Omit any personal anecdotes or filler.

[PASTE TRANSCRIPT]
```

### Key takeaways (for educational content)
```
Here is a lecture or tutorial transcript. List the 5 most important things to understand or remember from this video. Use plain language.

[PASTE TRANSCRIPT]
```

### Action items (for how-to or tutorial videos)
```
This is a transcript of a tutorial video. Extract a numbered step-by-step action list of everything I need to do to apply what was taught. Be specific and sequential.

[PASTE TRANSCRIPT]
```

### TL;DR (for long interviews or podcasts)
```
Summarize this transcript in 3 sentences or fewer. Assume the reader has no context. Just the most important point.

[PASTE TRANSCRIPT]
```

## What About Long Videos?

A 1-hour video produces roughly 8,000–12,000 words of transcript. ChatGPT's free tier (GPT-4o mini) handles transcripts up to about 10,000–15,000 words. For very long videos (2+ hours), you have two options:

**Option 1:** Use Claude (claude.ai) — it has a larger context window and handles very long transcripts well on its free tier.

**Option 2:** Summarize in parts. Paste the first half of the transcript with "Summarize this first half of a long transcript. I'll send more." Then paste the second half and ask for a combined summary.

## Tips for Better Summaries

**Be specific about what you want.** "Summarize this" gets a generic result. "Summarize this in 5 bullet points for someone who knows nothing about the topic" gets something much more useful.

**Ask follow-up questions.** After getting the summary, ask "What did the speaker say specifically about [X]?" or "Were there any statistics or data mentioned?" The AI still has the full transcript in context.

**Check against the original.** AI summaries are usually accurate but occasionally drop nuance or misread tone. For high-stakes use (research, business decisions), spot-check the summary against the source transcript.

**Download the transcript too.** Before you close YTTranscript, [download the full transcript](/how-to-download-youtube-transcript) as a DOCX or PDF. You'll have both the summary and the original text for reference.
