**For long YouTube videos, the best tool is one that reads the captions YouTube already made, not one that re-transcribes the audio.** A caption-based tool like [YTTranscript](https://yttranscript.app) returns a 3-hour podcast or a 10-hour livestream transcript in seconds. **For whole playlists, the honest answer depends on volume:** paste URLs one by one for a few dozen videos, or use a bulk tool like youtube-transcript.io or yt-dlp for hundreds.

Here is how the main options compare, and which one fits your job.

## Why long videos and playlists break most tools

Most transcription apps were built for meetings and uploaded files. They **charge by audio minute** and often cap free files at 30 minutes, so a 3-hour lecture means waiting for processing and burning paid minutes.

YouTube videos usually already have a caption track. A tool that retrieves that track does no audio processing at all, so length barely matters. The step-by-step version is in our guide to [transcribing a long YouTube video](/how-to-transcribe-a-long-youtube-video).

Playlists add a second problem: **repetition**. Twenty pastes is fine; 400 is a job for automation.

## 2. youtube-transcript.io — best for bulk playlists and automation

If you regularly pull **hundreds of transcripts**, youtube-transcript.io wins. Paid plans add playlist and CSV bulk jobs plus an API, so a 300-video playlist is one job instead of 300 pastes. The trade-off is a monthly subscription and an account. See our full [YTTranscript vs youtube-transcript.io](/yttranscript-vs-youtube-transcript-io) comparison.

## 3. yt-dlp — best free option for technical users

yt-dlp is a free command-line downloader that can save the subtitle files for every video in a playlist in one command. It's the cheapest route to bulk, but you get raw subtitle files (VTT/SRT) that need cleaning, and it breaks occasionally when YouTube changes. More in [yt-dlp vs YouTube transcript tools](/yt-dlp-vs-youtube-transcript-tools).

## 4. Whisper — best for long videos with no captions

OpenAI's Whisper runs locally and transcribes audio with no per-minute fee. It's the right call for caption-less archives if you have a capable machine; on a laptop CPU, a 3-hour file can take a long time. Compare in [YTTranscript vs Whisper](/yttranscript-vs-whisper).

## 5. Otter.ai and other upload tools — skip for YouTube

Otter.ai is excellent for meetings but can't take a YouTube URL, and its free plan caps each file at 30 minutes. For YouTube work it's slower and more expensive than a caption-based tool.
