## Which format should you download?

| Format | Best for | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **TXT** | Pasting into AI tools, quick editing | Smallest, most universal |
| **DOCX** | Editing in Word, formatting notes | Keeps it editable |
| **PDF** | Reading, printing, archiving, citing | Looks the same everywhere — see the [PDF guide](/youtube-transcript-to-pdf) |
| **SRT** | Adding captions to a video | Subtitle format — see the [SRT guide](/youtube-transcript-to-srt) |

Pick **TXT** if you're feeding the text to [ChatGPT](/youtube-transcript-for-chatgpt) or another AI tool, **PDF** for study notes and archiving, **DOCX** for editing, and **SRT** for captioning work.
