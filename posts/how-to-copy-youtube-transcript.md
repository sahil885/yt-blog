## The YouTube Built-in Method (and Why It's Worse)

YouTube does have its own transcript viewer. Here's how to access it — and why it's more work:

1. Open a YouTube video
2. Click the **three dots (⋯)** below the video
3. Click **Show transcript**
4. A transcript panel opens on the right side

**The problem:** There's no "Copy All" button. You have to manually click into the transcript, select all the text (`Cmd+A` or `Ctrl+A`), and copy it. The result usually includes timestamps interleaved with the text, making it messy to paste and use elsewhere. It also doesn't work on mobile.

YTTranscript gives you a clean copy in one click, with an option to strip timestamps.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Does it work on any YouTube video?**
It works on any public YouTube video that has captions enabled — which includes the vast majority of videos, since YouTube auto-generates captions for most content.

**What if a video has no transcript?**
If a video has no captions (live streams without recording, some older videos), no tool can extract a transcript. YTTranscript will let you know if no captions are available.

**Can I copy transcripts from YouTube Shorts?**
Yes — Shorts are regular YouTube videos with short URLs, and they work in YTTranscript the same way.

**Is there a word or character limit?**
No. YTTranscript copies the complete transcript regardless of video length.
