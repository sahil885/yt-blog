**DeepSeek** has become one of the most popular AI assistants for its strong reasoning at a low cost. A common question is whether it can summarize YouTube videos. The short answer: **yes — but only from a transcript.** DeepSeek's chat can't open a YouTube link or watch a video, so the trick is to give it the text. This guide shows you the free, reliable way to use **YouTube transcripts with DeepSeek**.

## The key thing about DeepSeek and YouTube

DeepSeek reads text, not video. The standard DeepSeek chat (web or app) **cannot access a YouTube URL** — paste a link and ask for a summary, and it will either guess from the title or tell you it can't open it. This is the same limitation [ChatGPT](/youtube-transcript-for-chatgpt) and [Claude](/youtube-transcript-with-claude) have.

The fix is simple: extract the video's **transcript** and paste it in. DeepSeek then has the complete text and can summarize, analyze, or rewrite it perfectly.

### Step 2: Paste it into DeepSeek with a prompt

Open DeepSeek, paste the transcript, and put your instruction **before** the text so it's clear what you want:

> "Summarize this YouTube video transcript in 5 bullet points: [paste transcript]"

> "Turn this transcript into structured study notes with headings: [paste transcript]"

> "List every actionable tip mentioned, in order: [paste transcript]"

### Step 3: Refine and reuse

Ask follow-ups — *"explain point 3 in simpler terms,"* *"translate the summary to Spanish,"* or *"rewrite this as a LinkedIn post."* Since DeepSeek has the full text, the answers stay accurate to the video.
