**Yes — you can get a full YouTube transcript on an iPhone, free, with no app to install.** Copy the video link from the YouTube app's **Share** sheet, open [yttranscript.app](https://yttranscript.app) in Safari, paste the link, and the full transcript appears. You can then copy it or save it to Files. The YouTube app has a built-in transcript view, but it's cramped and awkward to copy from — a web tool is faster and gives you exportable text.

Here's the exact flow on iOS.

## Step-by-step on iPhone

1. **Open the video** in the YouTube app (or in Safari).
2. **Tap Share, then Copy Link.** In the YouTube app: tap **Share** under the video, then **Copy link**. In Safari: tap the address bar and copy the URL.
3. **Open YTTranscript in Safari.** Go to [yttranscript.app](https://yttranscript.app).
4. **Paste the link and submit.** The tool reads the video's caption track and returns the full transcript.
5. **Copy or export.** Tap to copy the text, or export to TXT, DOCX, or PDF and save it to **Files**, **Notes**, or share it anywhere.

The same steps work on iPad and, with the Android share sheet, on Android too — see the general [transcript on mobile](/youtube-transcript-on-mobile) guide.

## One thing to know

A link-based tool reads YouTube's **caption track**, so the video needs captions (auto-generated or uploaded) for a transcript to exist. Almost all popular videos have them. If a specific video has no captions at all, see [what to do when there's no transcript](/youtube-video-no-transcript).
