## What is a YouTube transcript generator?

It's a tool that reads a video's captions and gives you the full text as one clean document. YouTube creates captions for almost every video — either typed by the creator (**manual**) or generated automatically by YouTube's speech recognition (**auto-generated**). A generator pulls that caption data and formats it into text you can actually use.
