**Happy Scribe** is a professional transcription and subtitling platform with translation, SRT/VTT exports, and team features. **YTTranscript** is a free tool that instantly turns any YouTube video into text. Both can handle a YouTube link, but they serve different needs. Here's an honest 2026 comparison.

## The core difference

YTTranscript is **free and instant**. Paste a YouTube URL, get the full text in 2-5 seconds, copy or download it. No account, no limits.

Happy Scribe is a **paid subtitling and transcription platform**. It accepts YouTube links too, but its strengths are professional outputs: accurate subtitles, SRT/VTT files, translation into many languages, and collaboration — wrapped in a subscription with a short free trial.

## When YTTranscript is the better choice

If you just want a video's **text** — to read, search, summarize, or feed to AI — YTTranscript is faster and free. There's no trial limit, no account, and no subscription. ## When Happy Scribe is the better choice

Happy Scribe earns its subscription when subtitling is the **product**, not a side task: precise, styled captions, professional **SRT/VTT** exports, **translation** into dozens of languages, and team collaboration. If you publish subtitled or localized video regularly, those features justify the cost in a way a free text tool can't match.

## Honest take

It comes down to text vs subtitles. For grabbing the words out of a YouTube video quickly and freely, YTTranscript is the simpler, cheaper choice. For polished, translated subtitle files and team workflows, Happy Scribe is purpose-built. Plenty of creators use YTTranscript for fast research and AI prompts, and reach for a paid platform only when a project needs broadcast-quality captions.

**Is YTTranscript or Happy Scribe better for YouTube transcripts?**
For quickly getting a YouTube video's text, YTTranscript is free, instant, and needs no signup. Happy Scribe is better for professional subtitling, SRT/VTT exports, and translation, which it offers on paid plans.

**How much does Happy Scribe cost?**
As of 2026, Happy Scribe offers a short free trial (about 10 minutes), then paid plans starting around $17/month for Basic, with Pro and Business tiers above that. An account is required.

**Is YTTranscript free?**
Yes, completely free with no account, no extension, and no limits. You paste a YouTube URL and get the transcript in seconds.

**Does Happy Scribe support YouTube links?**
Yes. Happy Scribe lets you paste a YouTube link for transcription, then translate and export as SRT or VTT — useful for subtitling workflows.

**Which should I choose?**
Choose YTTranscript for fast, free text. Choose Happy Scribe if you need polished subtitle files, translations, or team features and don't mind a subscription.
