Otter.ai is a **meeting transcription tool** — it's built for Zoom calls, Google Meet, voice recordings, and in-person conversations. Using it for YouTube is a workaround, not a feature.

YTTranscript is built specifically for YouTube. It extracts the transcript directly from YouTube's caption data in seconds. No audio re-processing, no minute limits, no workarounds.

## What Is Otter.ai?

Otter.ai is one of the most popular meeting transcription tools available. It integrates with Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams to automatically record and transcribe meetings. It's excellent for what it's designed for.

For YouTube, the workflow is awkward: you have to either play the video out loud while Otter.ai listens via microphone, or use a system audio capture setup. The resulting transcript quality depends on your audio setup and eats into your monthly transcription minutes.

- 300 transcription minutes per month
- Maximum 30 minutes per conversation
- Requires account and app/browser extension

## The Minute-Limit Problem

Otter.ai's free plan gives you 300 transcription minutes per month. A 1-hour YouTube video uses 60 of those minutes. If you're doing research across multiple long videos, you'll burn through your monthly allowance quickly.

YTTranscript has no such limit. Because it reads YouTube's existing caption data rather than re-transcribing audio, it doesn't consume any recording time. You can process as many videos as you need.

---

## Transcript Quality

When using Otter.ai for YouTube, transcription quality depends on your audio quality — background noise, speaker accents, and audio compression all affect the output. Errors are common.

YTTranscript uses YouTube's own captions, which are either manually created by the video author (highest quality) or auto-generated by Google's speech recognition (generally very accurate for clear audio). The result is identical to what YouTube itself shows in the transcript panel.
