**The best Tactiq alternative depends on which Tactiq you mean: for YouTube transcripts, YTTranscript is the closest replacement (no account, no extension, TXT/DOCX/PDF export); for meeting notes, Otter.ai and Fireflies.ai are the strongest options.** Tactiq is really two products — a free web tool that pulls YouTube transcripts, and a paid Chrome extension that transcribes Google Meet, Zoom and Microsoft Teams calls. Most people searching for "Tactiq alternatives" want a better version of one of those two, not both.

This guide splits the alternatives along that line, with pricing checked in October 2026, and says plainly which tool wins for which job.

## What Tactiq does well (and where it falls short)

Credit where it's due. Tactiq's **YouTube transcript generator** is free, needs no signup, and works by pasting a URL — it's a decent tool. Its meeting extension is genuinely useful too: it captures live captions in Meet, Zoom and Teams without sending a bot into the call, which some people prefer.

The common reasons people look elsewhere:

- **YouTube exports are TXT only.** No DOCX, no PDF.
- **The meeting product is Chrome-only.** No Safari, no Firefox, no phone.
- **The free meeting plan is tight:** **10 transcripts and 5 AI credits per month**.
- **Paid plans are per user:** Pro is **$8/user/month billed annually**, Team **$16.67**, Business **$29.17** (Tactiq's pricing page, October 2026).

### 2. Eightify — best if you only want a summary

**Eightify** skips the full transcript and gives you an AI summary with key insights. ### 3. YouTube's built-in "Show transcript"

YouTube already shows transcripts under the description on desktop and in the mobile app. It's free and instant, but there's **no download button** — you copy and paste, then clean up timestamps manually. Fine for one quote; tedious for anything longer. See [how to copy a YouTube transcript](/how-to-copy-youtube-transcript).

## Best Tactiq alternatives for meetings

### 4. Otter.ai — best free meeting tier

Otter's free Basic plan gives **300 minutes per month with a 30-minute cap per conversation**, and it has real iOS and Android apps — something Tactiq's extension can't offer. The trade-off is that Otter sends an assistant into your calls, which not every team is comfortable with. More in [Otter alternatives](/otter-ai-alternatives).

### 5. Fireflies.ai — best for sales and CRM teams

**Fireflies** records via a notetaker bot, exports DOCX, PDF, SRT and more, and pushes summaries into CRMs and Slack. Paid plans start around **$10/user/month billed annually**. It's more powerful than Tactiq for teams, and more than an individual usually needs. See [YTTranscript vs Fireflies.ai](/yttranscript-vs-fireflies-ai).
