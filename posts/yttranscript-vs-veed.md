**If you just want the words out of a YouTube video, YTTranscript is the faster tool; if you need to edit the video itself or burn styled captions onto it, VEED is the right choice.** VEED is a browser-based video editor that happens to do auto-subtitles. [YTTranscript](https://yttranscript.app) is a dedicated transcript tool: paste a YouTube link, get the full text, export it, done — no account, no install, no watermark. They overlap on one feature and diverge on everything else.

## What VEED Is Actually For

VEED is a video production tool. You bring in footage, cut it, add music, add B-roll, generate captions, style them, translate them, and export a finished video. Auto-subtitling is one feature inside a much larger editor, and VEED markets high accuracy across a very wide language set.

That breadth is a genuine strength. If you are a creator who publishes video and needs **burned-in captions**, **translated subtitles**, or **SRT files to upload to YouTube Studio**, VEED does all of that in one place. No transcript-only tool competes with that.

The trade-offs are equally real:

- **You need an account.** There's no anonymous, paste-and-go path.
- **The free tier is a trial, not a workflow.** Exports carry a VEED watermark, export length is capped, and monthly AI minutes are limited.
- **Subtitle file downloads live behind the paywall.** SRT, VTT, and TXT exports require a paid plan.
- **It's oriented around your own video.** VEED is built to process footage you upload or own — it isn't a "paste any public YouTube link and read the transcript" tool.
- **It's slower for text.** Import, process, then export is several steps more than pasting a URL.

## The Accuracy Question

VEED runs speech recognition on the audio and advertises very high accuracy. YTTranscript reads YouTube's own caption track. That distinction matters more than any percentage claim:

- On videos with **creator-uploaded (manual) captions**, YouTube's track is typically excellent — often better than machine transcription, because a human wrote it. See [auto-generated vs manual transcripts](/youtube-auto-generated-vs-manual-transcript).
- On videos with **auto-generated captions**, quality tracks the audio: clean studio audio transcribes well; heavy accents, crosstalk, and jargon degrade it — for both tools.
- On videos with **no captions at all**, YTTranscript has nothing to read. VEED — or [Whisper](/yttranscript-vs-whisper) — wins outright here. That's the honest limit. If you hit it, see [what to do when a YouTube video has no transcript](/youtube-video-no-transcript).

## The Verdict

**Use VEED if** you are producing video and need captions as part of that production: styled on-screen captions, translated subtitles, SRT files for upload, or editing and transcription in a single tool. Budget for a paid plan — the free tier's watermark and export caps make it impractical for real work.

**Use YTTranscript if** you want the text of a YouTube video and nothing else: research notes, quotes for an article, [a transcript to paste into ChatGPT](/youtube-transcript-for-chatgpt), study material, or a draft source for [turning a video into a blog post](/youtube-video-to-blog-post). It's dramatically faster for that job, it doesn't ask for an account, and it works from a phone.

**They aren't really competitors.** Plenty of people use both: YTTranscript to pull text out of videos they're researching, VEED to caption the videos they publish.

If your job is text, start with [YTTranscript](https://yttranscript.app). If your job is video, start with VEED.

## FAQ

**Is VEED or YTTranscript better for getting a YouTube transcript?**
If you only want the text of a YouTube video, YTTranscript is faster — paste a URL, get the transcript, no account and no watermark. VEED is better when you also need to edit the video, burn styled captions onto it, or translate subtitles into other languages.

**Can VEED transcribe a YouTube video from a URL?**
VEED lets you bring in a video by URL for content you own, then run auto-subtitles on it. It is designed around uploading and editing your own footage, not around pulling text out of someone else's public video.

**Does VEED have a free plan for subtitles?**
VEED has a free tier, but it is limited — exports carry a VEED watermark, export length is capped, and AI/auto-subtitle minutes are restricted each month. Subtitle file downloads such as SRT, VTT, and TXT sit on paid plans.

**How much does VEED cost?**
At the time of writing, VEED's published tiers run roughly $18/month for Basic, $30/month for Pro, and $70/month for Business, with lower rates on annual billing. Check VEED's pricing page before buying — editor pricing changes often.

**Is YTTranscript completely free?**
YTTranscript has a free tier plus a paid option. It never requires a signup, an install, or a browser extension, and exports are not watermarked.
