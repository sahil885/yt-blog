**Descript** is a well-known audio and video editor built around the transcript — you edit media by editing text. **YTTranscript** is a free tool that does one thing: turn any YouTube video into text instantly. They overlap on transcription, but they're built for different jobs. Here's an honest comparison for 2026.

## The core difference

YTTranscript is a **free, no-signup transcript tool**. Paste a YouTube URL, get the full text in seconds, copy or download it. That's the whole product, and it's free with no limits.

Descript is a **paid editing suite**. Transcription is the entry point, but the real value is editing your podcast or video by editing the transcript, removing filler words, and applying AI features. It's powerful — and priced and structured accordingly, with an account and a limited free tier.

## When Descript is the better choice

If transcription is one step in a bigger project — editing a podcast, cutting a video, removing "ums," or producing polished media — Descript earns its price. It's a genuine production tool, not just a transcript grabber. If you mainly want to **edit media**, Descript is the right category of product; if you mainly want **text**, it's more than you need.

## Honest take

These tools aren't really competitors so much as different categories. YTTranscript is the fast, free way to get a video's text. Descript is a paid editor where the transcript is the editing canvas. Many people actually use both: grab quick transcripts with YTTranscript for research and AI prompts, and reserve Descript for the videos they're actually editing.

**Can Descript transcribe a YouTube video?**
Yes. Descript can transcribe videos and has a free YouTube-to-text tool, but its core product is a paid audio/video editor built around the transcript.

**Which should I choose?**
Choose YTTranscript if you just need the text fast and free. Choose Descript if transcription is one step in a larger podcast or video editing workflow.
