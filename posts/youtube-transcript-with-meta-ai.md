## Why Meta AI Can't Summarize YouTube Videos Directly

Meta AI is built on Meta's Llama models, and like ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini it is fundamentally a **text model**. It reads and writes language extremely well, but it has no reliable way to stream a YouTube video, listen to the audio, or read the on-screen captions. Even when an assistant appears to "open" a link, it is usually guessing from the title and a snippet of metadata — not the real content.

That is why the results are so much better when you hand Meta AI the **full transcript**. Instead of guessing, it works from the exact words spoken in the video. The only missing piece is a fast, free way to turn a video into clean text — which is exactly what YTTranscript does.

## Best Prompts to Use with Meta AI

The quality of your summary depends heavily on the prompt. Paste the transcript first, then try one of these:

- **Quick summary:** *"Summarize this transcript in 5 bullet points, then give a one-sentence takeaway."*
- **Study notes:** *"Turn this transcript into structured study notes with headings and sub-points."*
- **Action items:** *"Extract every task, recommendation, or action item mentioned in this transcript."*
- **Key quotes:** *"List the 5 most important direct quotes from this transcript."*
- **Explain simply:** *"Explain the main idea of this transcript as if I'm a beginner."*

Because Meta AI keeps context within a chat, you can keep asking follow-up questions — *"What did they say about pricing?"* — and it will answer from the transcript you pasted.
