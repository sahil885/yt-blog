**The fastest free way to generate YouTube chapters is to copy your video's timestamped transcript, paste it into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini with a chaptering prompt, and paste the result into your description.** YouTube only turns that list into chapters if it starts at **00:00**, has **at least three timestamps in ascending order**, and every chapter lasts **at least 10 seconds**. YouTube can also add automatic chapters, but not every video is eligible, and any list you write replaces them.

Here is the full method, the rules, and how it compares with the other options.

## YouTube's chapter rules

From YouTube's Help Center:

- Add timestamps and titles to the **video description** in YouTube Studio.
- The first timestamp must be **00:00**.
- You need **three or more** timestamps, in ascending order.
- Each chapter must be **at least 10 seconds** long.
- Your own list **overrides automatic chapters**.
- Chapters require access to YouTube's **advanced features**, and aren't available if the channel has active strikes or the content may be inappropriate for some viewers.

## Four ways to create chapters, compared

| Method | Effort per video | Cost | Control over titles | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Manual** | Highest: you rewatch and note times | Free | Full | Short videos with obvious sections |
| **YouTube automatic chapters** | None | Free | None: replace them with your own list | Channels that won't write chapters |
| **Transcript + AI** (YTTranscript + ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini) | About 2 minutes | Free tier (2 transcripts/day) | Full: edit the AI's draft | Most creators |
| **Dedicated AI chapter tools** (e.g. Gling, TimeSkip, ScreenApp) | About a minute | Varies by tool | Medium | High-volume channels |

The transcript method wins for most people because the AI works from **real timestamps in your transcript**, and you keep full control over the titles.

YouTube's minimum is 10 seconds, but 60-second chapters are more useful to viewers. Very long transcripts may need splitting into halves.

**Step 4: Paste into YouTube Studio.** Open **Content**, select the video, add the list to the description and click **Save**.

## Write chapter titles that help search

Chapters can show up outside YouTube. Google Search may display timestamps from a YouTube description as **key moments** in results, and Google says it prefers timestamps creators mark explicitly. Its guidelines: put each timestamp on its own line, keep the label on the same line, list them in chronological order, and give every label at least one word.

So write titles for a searcher: "How to set up SSO" beats "Part 3". Put the key phrase first, keep titles short, and match the words your audience actually types. More in [YouTube transcripts for SEO](/youtube-transcript-for-seo).

## Chapters not showing? Check these

- The first timestamp isn't **00:00**.
- There are fewer than three timestamps, or they're out of order.
- One chapter is shorter than 10 seconds.
- Your channel doesn't have advanced features enabled yet.
- The channel has an active strike, or the video may be inappropriate for some viewers.

## Frequently asked questions

**What are the requirements for YouTube chapters?**
Add the timestamps to your video description. The first timestamp must be 00:00, you need at least three timestamps in ascending order, and each chapter must be at least 10 seconds long. A manual list overrides YouTube's automatic chapters.

**Can ChatGPT create YouTube timestamps?**
Yes, if you give it the transcript. ChatGPT can't watch a YouTube video, so paste the video's timestamped transcript and ask for chapters that start at 00:00. Check each timestamp against the video before you publish.

**Why are my YouTube chapters not showing?**
Usually the list breaks a rule: it doesn't start at 00:00, has fewer than three timestamps, isn't in ascending order, or has a chapter under 10 seconds. Chapters also require access to YouTube's advanced features and are unavailable if the channel has active strikes or the content may be inappropriate for some viewers.

**Are YouTube automatic chapters good enough?**
They're free and take no work, but not every video is eligible, you can't edit their titles, and any list you add yourself replaces them. Most creators get clearer, more searchable titles by writing their own.

**Do YouTube chapters help SEO?**
They can. Google Search may show timestamps from a YouTube description as key moments in search results, and Google says it prefers timestamps that creators mark explicitly, one per line, each with a label.
