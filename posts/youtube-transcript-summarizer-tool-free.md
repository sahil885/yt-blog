## An honest caveat on accuracy

Free AI summaries are excellent for gists and key points, but they can miss nuance, compress away caveats, or misattribute a claim to the wrong speaker. For anything you'll **publish or cite**, keep the full transcript open and check the summary against it. This is exactly why getting the transcript first — not just a summary — is worth the extra step for serious work.
