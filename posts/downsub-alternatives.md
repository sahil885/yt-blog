## DownSub not working?

Match the cause to the fix:

- **The site is down or the download button does nothing.** Try again later, or get the same YouTube transcript from [YTTranscript](https://yttranscript.app) or YouTube's own **Show transcript** panel.
- **"No subtitles found" on a YouTube video.** The video has no captions, so no caption-based downloader (DownSub, SaveSubs, YTTranscript's free tier or yt-dlp) can return any. You need a tool that transcribes the audio; see [what to do when a YouTube video has no transcript](/youtube-video-no-transcript).
- **A streaming site (Viki, WeTV, iQIYI) fails or is region-locked.** Coverage on these sites changes often. Try SaveSubs, or yt-dlp, which supports thousands of sites from the command line.
- **Your network or browser blocks the site.** Work and school networks sometimes block downloader sites. A plain transcript site like YTTranscript, or YouTube's built-in panel, is usually still reachable.

## Why people look for DownSub alternatives

DownSub does its core job well, so the reasons to switch are usually specific:

- **You want a document, not subtitle cues.** SRT and VTT files are split into short timed lines, built for video players. Reading, quoting or feeding a transcript to AI works better as clean paragraphs.
- **The feature you need is behind Pro.** Playlist downloads, AI translation and summaries are paid features.
- **You only use YouTube.** If you never touch Viki or WeTV, a YouTube-focused tool can give you a simpler workflow and better export options.

## 2. SaveSubs: best free multi-site subtitle downloader

SaveSubs downloads, converts and edits subtitles from YouTube, Viki, Facebook, Dailymotion and other sites, and says it is free and always will be. It's the closest like-for-like DownSub replacement when you need subtitle files from more than YouTube.

## 3. yt-dlp: best for bulk and scripting

The open-source command-line tool **yt-dlp** can download subtitles without downloading the video, for example `yt-dlp --write-subs --write-auto-subs --sub-langs en --skip-download URL`. It supports thousands of sites, handles whole playlists and costs nothing, but it has no interface. Our [yt-dlp vs transcript tools guide](/yt-dlp-vs-youtube-transcript-tools) covers when the setup is worth it.

## 4. YouTube's built-in transcript: best for one-offs

On any captioned video, expand the description and select **Show transcript**, on desktop or in the mobile app. It's free and instant, but there's no download button: you copy and paste. Details in [how to copy a YouTube transcript](/how-to-copy-youtube-transcript).

## 5. youtube-transcript.io: best for bulk YouTube and API access

youtube-transcript.io includes 25 free transcripts a month, then subscriptions from **$9.99/month for 1,000 transcripts** (Pro is $24.99/month for 3,000), with bulk playlist jobs, an API and TXT, SRT, VTT or CSV exports. Full breakdown in [YTTranscript vs youtube-transcript.io](/yttranscript-vs-youtube-transcript-io).

## 6. NoteGPT: best for a transcript plus an AI summary

NoteGPT's free YouTube transcript generator needs no login, copies or downloads TXT with or without timestamps, and can AI-generate transcripts for videos that lack them. It's built around AI summaries and notes, with paid plans for heavier AI use. Compare in [YTTranscript vs NoteGPT](/yttranscript-vs-notegpt).

## 7. Tactiq: best if you already use Tactiq

Tactiq's free YouTube transcript tool lets you view, copy and download transcript text without entering an email. Tactiq's main product is a meeting transcription extension for Google Meet, Zoom, Teams and Webex, so the YouTube tool is a handy extra rather than a full subtitle workflow. See [YTTranscript vs Tactiq](/yttranscript-vs-tactiq).
