YouTube is one of the largest educational resources on the planet. But watching videos passively means you'll forget most of it within a week.

This guide shows how students can use YouTube transcripts to learn more effectively, in less time.

## Why Transcripts Make You a Better Student

Instead of taking notes while watching, you can:
1. Watch the video once for the big picture
2. Get the full transcript instantly
3. Annotate and extract key points at your own pace

## Study Techniques

**Flashcards:** Paste the transcript into ChatGPT and ask it to create 10 Q&A pairs. Import into Anki or Quizlet.

**Outline:** Ask ChatGPT to structure the lecture into main topics and subtopics.

**Key terms:** Use Ctrl+F to search the transcript for specific concepts.

**Share with study groups:** Paste into a shared Google Doc and divide sections to summarise.
