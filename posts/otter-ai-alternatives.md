**The best Otter.ai alternative depends on what you are transcribing.** For live meetings, Fireflies.ai is the closest like-for-like swap and its free plan includes unlimited transcription. For bulk audio and video files, TurboScribe is the cheapest at $10/month billed annually. For **YouTube videos specifically, Otter is the wrong tool entirely** — it cannot accept a YouTube URL, so a link-based tool like [YTTranscript](https://yttranscript.app) gets you the text in seconds instead of minutes.

Otter.ai is a good product with a narrow shape: it is built to sit in your Zoom, Meet or Teams call and transcribe live speech. People start hunting for alternatives when they hit one of three walls.

## Why people leave Otter.ai

**The free tier is tight.** Otter's free plan gives you **300 minutes per month**, capped at **30 minutes per conversation**, plus only **three file imports for the lifetime of the account**. That last limit is the one that surprises people — if you want to transcribe existing recordings rather than live calls, you get three, ever.

**It does not take URLs.** Otter has no way to accept a YouTube, Vimeo or podcast link. You must download a file first, then spend one of your import credits.

**Exports are limited on free.** Free users get fewer export formats and no advanced collaboration features.

None of that makes Otter bad. It makes it specialised. The question is which alternative matches your actual job.

### 1. Fireflies.ai — the closest free swap for meetings

Fireflies is the alternative most Otter users actually want. Every plan, **including Free, includes unlimited transcription**, with unlimited AI summaries and AskFred. Paid tiers start at **$10/seat/month billed annually** ($18 monthly).

The catch is AI credits. Pro includes 20/month and Business 30; once you exhaust them, AskFred, Soundbites and AI Skills pause until the next cycle or you buy more. Budget accordingly if you lean on the AI layer.

**Best for:** teams that want unlimited meeting transcripts without paying.

### 3. TurboScribe — cheapest for bulk files

TurboScribe's free plan allows **3 files per day, 30 minutes each**. The Unlimited plan is **$10/month billed annually ($20 monthly)** and removes the daily cap, supports files up to 10 hours, and handles up to 50 files at once. Pricing is refreshingly simple: one free tier, one paid tier, no seats or add-ons.

**Best for:** people with a folder full of long recordings. More detail in our [TurboScribe comparison](/yttranscript-vs-turboscribe).

### 4. Tactiq — lightweight live capture

Tactiq is a Chrome extension that captures live meeting transcripts in Google Meet, Zoom and Teams. Free gives you **10 transcripts and 5 AI credits per month**. It is lighter than Otter and faster to set up, but it is extension-dependent and meeting-only. See [YTTranscript vs Tactiq](/yttranscript-vs-tactiq).

**Best for:** solo users who want quick meeting transcripts inside the browser.

### 5. Notta — multilingual meetings

Notta's free plan is restrictive — **120 minutes per month with a 3-minute cap per recording**, which is demo-sized rather than meeting-sized. Pro is **$8.17/month billed annually ($13.99 monthly)** with 1,800 minutes and up to 5 hours per recording. Its strength is language coverage.

**Best for:** teams working across languages. For non-English video, see [non-English YouTube transcripts](/youtube-transcript-non-english).

### 6. Descript — transcription as an editing surface

Descript transcribes so you can edit the video by editing the text. The free plan is thin: **1 hour of transcription per month**, 720p exports with a watermark. Hobbyist is $16/user/month annually ($24 monthly); Creator is $24/user/month annually ($35 monthly) with 30 hours.

**Best for:** podcasters and video editors, not note-takers. Full breakdown in [YTTranscript vs Descript](/yttranscript-vs-descript).

### 7. Whisper — free, accurate, technical

OpenAI's Whisper is open source and genuinely free if you run it yourself. It is the honest winner in one specific case: **audio that has no captions at all**. It costs you setup time, compute and command-line comfort. See [YTTranscript vs Whisper](/yttranscript-vs-whisper).

**Best for:** developers and anyone transcribing uncaptioned audio.

## The verdict

- **Replacing Otter for meetings, free:** Fireflies.ai — unlimited transcription on the free plan.
- **Replacing Otter for meetings, paid:** Fireflies at $10/seat/month annually, or Tactiq if you want something lighter.
- **Bulk audio and video files:** TurboScribe at $10/month annually.
- **YouTube videos:** [YTTranscript](https://yttranscript.app) — the only option here that takes a URL directly, with no signup or extension.
- **Video editing:** Descript.
- **Uncaptioned audio, zero budget, technical:** Whisper.

**Stay with Otter** if you live in live meetings, are inside its free limits, and value its assistant and collaboration features. It is a reasonable product — it is just not a YouTube tool.
