## Why convert YouTube to text?

**It's faster than watching.** You can read a 30-minute video in a few minutes and jump straight to the parts you need.

**It's searchable.** Use Ctrl+F to find any word or quote instantly — impossible inside a video.

**It's reusable.** Text can be summarized by [ChatGPT](/youtube-transcript-for-chatgpt) or [Claude](/youtube-transcript-with-claude), saved to [Notion](/youtube-transcript-to-notion) or [Google Docs](/youtube-transcript-to-google-docs), or turned into a [blog post](/youtube-video-to-blog-post).

**It's accessible.** Text works with screen readers and can be translated into any language — see [non-English transcripts](/youtube-transcript-non-english).
