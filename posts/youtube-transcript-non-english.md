## Translating a Non-English Transcript to English

Once you have the transcript in the original language, translating it is straightforward using free AI tools.

**Method 1: Use ChatGPT or Claude**

Paste the foreign language transcript with this prompt:

```
Here is a transcript in [LANGUAGE]. Please translate it to English, maintaining the meaning and tone. Preserve paragraph breaks and structure.

[PASTE TRANSCRIPT]
```

**Method 2: Use Google Translate**

Go to translate.google.com, paste the transcript, and set the target language to English. Works well for transcripts under 5,000 characters.

**Method 3: Use DeepL**

DeepL (deepl.com) is considered the most accurate machine translation tool for European languages. If you're translating Spanish, French, German, Italian, or Portuguese, DeepL often produces more natural results.

## What Languages Does YouTube Auto-Caption Support?

## Tips for Non-English Transcripts

**Check auto-caption quality before using.** Open the video in YouTube and check "Show transcript" to preview the captions. If they look heavily mangled, the auto-ASR struggled with this video.

**For language learning, leave timestamps on.** Keep timestamps so you can play back specific sections while reading the text. See our guide on [YouTube transcripts with timestamps](/youtube-transcript-with-timestamps).
