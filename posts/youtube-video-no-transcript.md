**If a YouTube video has no "Show transcript" option, it has no captions, so no caption-based tool can copy a transcript from it; you need a tool that transcribes the audio instead.** [YTTranscript](https://yttranscript.app) can make an AI transcript from the audio for about 10 credits (this needs a pack, from $9 for 100), and free options exist too: OpenAI's Whisper on your own computer, or the limited free plans of TurboScribe and NoteGPT. If the video is new, check again in a few hours first: YouTube's own auto-captions may simply not be ready yet.

*Last updated: November 2026.*

## Why some videos have no "Show transcript" button

The button only appears when a video has a caption track. It goes missing when:

- **The video is new.** Auto-captions can take a few hours to appear, longer for long videos and live-stream replays.
- **The creator turned captions off** in YouTube Studio and didn't upload their own.
- **The audio isn't speech YouTube can caption:** mostly music, heavy background noise, or a very poor recording.
- **The language isn't auto-captioned.** YouTube auto-captions many languages but not all of them.
- **The video is private or age-restricted,** so outside tools can't read it even if captions exist.

To check: on a computer, click **...more** under the video. If there's no Transcript section, and the **CC** button in the player is greyed out, the video has no captions.

## Option 2: Whisper-based tools

OpenAI's **Whisper** is a free, open-source speech-to-text model, and many transcription sites are built on it.

- **Run Whisper yourself (free).** Install it on your computer, get the audio of a video you're allowed to download (your own, or one with a permissive licence), and run it. It's accurate on clear speech but slow on long files without a good graphics card, and it takes some technical setup. See [YTTranscript vs Whisper](/yttranscript-vs-whisper).
- **TurboScribe (free tier, then paid).** Paste the YouTube link or upload the file. The free plan allows 3 files a day of up to 30 minutes each, with a free account; longer videos need Unlimited ($10/month billed yearly, or $20 month to month). See [TurboScribe for YouTube vs YTTranscript](/yttranscript-vs-turboscribe).

## Option 3: Upload the audio to a transcription service

If you own the video, download it from **YouTube Studio** and upload the audio to any transcription service (TurboScribe, NoteGPT, Otter and others). This is also the only route for private videos, since outside tools can't open them.

Costs to expect: free tiers are usually capped by minutes or files a day, and paid plans run roughly $5–$20 a month (see the table). For one-off jobs, a one-time option or a free tier is usually enough.

## Option 4: Ask the creator

If you need an accurate transcript of someone else's video, ask the creator to upload captions. Many creators have a script or caption file already, and adding it helps every viewer.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why doesn't my YouTube video have a Show transcript button?**
The video has no captions. Either the creator turned them off and didn't upload any, YouTube hasn't finished making auto-captions for a new video, or the audio is mostly music, noise or a language YouTube doesn't auto-caption.

**How do I transcribe a YouTube video without captions?**
Use a tool that transcribes the audio itself. YTTranscript can make an AI transcript for about 10 credits (this needs a pack), TurboScribe and NoteGPT have free allowances with limits, and OpenAI's Whisper is free if you run it on your own computer.

**Is there a free way to transcribe a YouTube video with no captions?**
Yes, with limits. Whisper is free and open source but you run it yourself. TurboScribe's free plan allows 3 files a day of up to 30 minutes each, and NoteGPT's 15 free monthly quotas cover about 150 minutes of caption-less video.

**Can ChatGPT transcribe a YouTube video with no captions?**
No. ChatGPT doesn't listen to the video from a link. In our November 2026 test it would summarize a link but refused to give a full verbatim transcript even when captions existed.

**Will a transcript tool work on a private or age-restricted video?**
Usually not. Transcript tools only read public videos. For a private video you own, download the audio from YouTube Studio and upload it to a transcription service.
