## Why You'd Want a Transcript Without Timestamps

Timestamps are clutter when your goal is the content itself. A **clean transcript** is what you want when you're:

- Pasting the text into **ChatGPT or Claude** for a summary (timestamps waste tokens and confuse the model)
- Turning a video into a **blog post**, newsletter, or script
- **Quoting** a passage in an article or essay
- Reading along or studying without timecodes breaking every line

In all these cases, the time markers add noise and force you to clean up before you can use the text.

## Already Have a Transcript With Timestamps? Remove Them

If you've already pulled a transcript that has timecodes (for example, from YouTube's panel), here are two fast ways to strip them.

**Option A — Ask an AI to clean it.** Paste the timestamped text into **ChatGPT** or **Claude** with a prompt like:

> "Remove all timestamps from this transcript and return it as clean paragraphs, keeping every word of the original."

**Option B — Find-and-replace.** In any text editor that supports regular expressions, replace the timestamp pattern (e.g. `\d{1,2}:\d{2}`) with nothing. The timecodes vanish and the words remain.
