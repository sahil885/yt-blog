**Fireflies.ai and YTTranscript both produce text from video, but they are built for different jobs.** Fireflies is a meeting assistant — it joins your Zoom, Google Meet and Teams calls, records them, and produces transcripts, summaries and action items for a team. YTTranscript is a YouTube-first tool: paste a video URL, get the transcript in seconds, export it, done. If your work is mostly YouTube videos, YTTranscript is the faster path. If your work is mostly live calls, Fireflies is the better investment.

## What Each Tool Is Actually For

Fireflies.ai sits in your calendar. It sends a notetaker bot into scheduled meetings, captures the audio, and pushes summaries into CRMs and Slack. It also has an **Uploads** feature that accepts YouTube videos and audio files in MP3, M4A, WAV, MP4 and WEBM — uploads are capped at 150 minutes each. So YouTube transcription works, it just isn't the product's centre of gravity.

YTTranscript does one thing: it pulls the transcript from a YouTube URL. There's no bot, no calendar connection, no upload wait, and no signup. That narrowness is the point — for the specific task of "I need the text of this video," fewer steps wins.

## Speed: The Real Difference

The gap shows up in the number of steps. With YTTranscript you copy the video URL, paste it, and read or download the transcript — see [how to get a YouTube transcript](/how-to-get-a-youtube-transcript) for the full walkthrough. With Fireflies you log in, open Uploads, upload or link the video, wait for processing, then open the transcript and download it — and on the Free plan, downloads are limited.

For one video that difference is small. For twenty videos of competitor research or a course playlist, it compounds.

## Where Fireflies Genuinely Wins

Being honest about this matters:

- **Meetings.** Nothing in YTTranscript touches live calls. Fireflies is purpose-built for them.
- **Videos with no captions.** Fireflies runs its own speech recognition, so it works on audio that has never been captioned. YTTranscript depends on YouTube's caption data — if a video has captions fully disabled, see [what to do when a YouTube video has no transcript](/youtube-video-no-transcript).
- **Speaker separation.** Fireflies produces reliable speaker labels. YouTube captions rarely include them; see [YouTube transcripts with speaker labels](/youtube-transcript-with-speaker-labels).
- **Built-in AI summaries and CRM push.** Fireflies generates summaries, action items and syncs them to your sales stack automatically.
- **Team workflows.** Shared workspaces, search across an org's meeting history, admin controls.

## Pricing, Honestly

Fireflies' Free plan includes unlimited transcription, which sounds generous — the constraint is elsewhere: 400 minutes of stored recordings per team, a 2-hour per-meeting recording limit, and blocked transcript downloads. Paid plans start around **$10/user/month billed annually** (roughly 40% more month-to-month), with Business tiers near $19/user/month. Per-seat pricing adds up fast for a team.

YTTranscript has a **free tier plus a paid option** for heavier use. It is not unlimited and it is not a full transcription suite — but for YouTube work it costs nothing to start and requires no seat licence.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can Fireflies.ai transcribe a YouTube video?**
Yes. Fireflies lets you upload a YouTube video file or add it through its Uploads feature, and uploads can be up to 150 minutes long. But YouTube is not its main use case — Fireflies is designed to join and transcribe live meetings.

**Which is better for YouTube transcripts, YTTranscript or Fireflies.ai?**
For YouTube specifically, YTTranscript is faster because you paste a URL and get text in seconds with no account, no bot and no upload step. Fireflies is the better tool if most of your transcription is Zoom, Meet or Teams calls.

**Does Fireflies.ai have a free plan?**
Yes. Fireflies has a free plan with unlimited transcription but only 400 minutes of storage per team, a 2-hour per-meeting recording cap, and restrictions on transcript downloads. Paid plans start around $10 per user per month billed annually.

**Do I need to create an account to use YTTranscript?**
No. YTTranscript works without a signup and without a browser extension — you paste a YouTube URL and get the transcript. It has a free tier plus a paid option for heavier use.

**Can Fireflies.ai handle a video that has no captions?**
Yes. Fireflies runs its own speech-to-text engine, so it can transcribe audio that has no existing captions. YTTranscript relies on YouTube's caption data, so a video with captions fully disabled needs a different approach.
