**YTTranscript and Recall solve different problems.** YTTranscript gives you the **full, word-for-word transcript** of a YouTube video in seconds — no account, no extension — and exports it as TXT, DOCX or PDF. **Recall** is an **AI knowledge base**: it summarizes videos, articles and PDFs, then tags and links them in a personal library you sign in to. Pick YTTranscript if you need the actual text; pick Recall if you want summaries organized over time.

## What is Recall?

Recall (now at recall.it) is a "save everything, summarize it, connect it" tool. You save a YouTube video through its browser extension (Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox), its web app, or its iOS and Android apps. Recall generates a short or detailed **AI summary with clickable timestamps**, files it in your knowledge base, and — on paid plans — auto-tags it, links it to related notes in a knowledge graph, and lets you chat with your saved content.

It's a strong product for people who consume a lot of material and want one searchable second brain. It is not primarily a transcript downloader.

## What is YTTranscript?

[YTTranscript](https://yttranscript.app) does one job: you paste a YouTube URL and get the **complete transcript** — with or without [timestamps](/youtube-transcript-with-timestamps) — then copy it or download it as **TXT, DOCX or PDF**. It runs in any browser, including on your phone, with **no signup and no extension**. The free tier covers 2 transcripts a day, and one-time packs (no subscription) cover heavier use.

## Where Recall wins

Be clear-eyed here: **Recall does more than YTTranscript**, it just does something different.

- **Cross-source knowledge base.** If you read articles, PDFs and watch videos on the same topic, Recall connects them. YTTranscript only touches YouTube.
- **Summaries without the reading.** A detailed Recall summary is faster to skim than a 9,000-word transcript of a two-hour podcast.
- **Chat and resurfacing.** Plus users can ask questions across their whole library and get spaced-repetition-style review. That's a learning tool, not a transcript tool.

If your goal is "remember what I watched," Recall is the better fit.

## Where YTTranscript wins

- **You get the source text, not an interpretation.** Summaries compress and sometimes drop the one line you needed. Journalists, [researchers](/youtube-transcript-for-researchers) and [students](/youtube-transcript-for-students) citing a video need verbatim quotes.
- **No account, nothing to install.** Open the site on a work laptop, a library computer or your phone and you're done in seconds. Recall asks you to sign up before it's useful.
- **Real files.** DOCX and PDF exports drop straight into Word, [Google Docs](/youtube-transcript-to-google-docs) or a client folder. Recall keeps content inside Recall.
- **Bring your own AI.** A full transcript works in any model you already pay for — [ChatGPT](/youtube-transcript-for-chatgpt), [Claude](/youtube-transcript-with-claude), [Gemini](/youtube-transcript-with-gemini) or [NotebookLM](/youtube-transcript-for-notebooklm) — with your own prompts, instead of a fixed summary style.
- **No subscription.** Recall's free plan caps you at 10 summaries a month; after that it's a yearly plan. YTTranscript's free tier is 2 transcripts a day, and one-time packs never become a recurring bill.

## Honest limitations of each

**YTTranscript** relies on the video's caption track. If a video has [no captions at all](/youtube-video-no-transcript), it can't produce a transcript — an audio transcription tool like [Whisper](/yttranscript-vs-whisper) is the better choice there. It also doesn't summarize on its own; you paste the text into an AI or use our [summarizer guide](/summarize-youtube-video-free).

**Recall** is summary-first. If you need every word, a summary isn't a substitute, and the free plan's 10 summaries a month go quickly for daily users. Your content also lives inside Recall rather than as files you control.

## Verdict: which should you choose?

- **Choose YTTranscript** if you need the **exact transcript** — for quoting, citing, editing into a [blog post](/youtube-video-to-blog-post), translating, or pasting into your own AI tool — and you want it without an account or subscription.
- **Choose Recall** if you want a **personal knowledge base** that summarizes and connects videos, articles and PDFs, and you're happy to sign up and pay yearly for heavy use.
- **Use both** if you're a heavy learner: save to Recall for the overview, grab the full transcript from YTTranscript when you need the words.
