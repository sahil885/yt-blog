**The best Notta alternative depends on what you transcribe.** For **meetings**, Fathom's free plan records and transcribes unlimited calls, and Fireflies' free plan advertises unlimited transcription. For **uploaded audio and video files**, TurboScribe's Unlimited plan at $10/month (billed yearly) is the cheapest heavy-use option. For **YouTube videos**, YTTranscript turns a link into a transcript in seconds with no account. Notta itself caps its free plan at 120 minutes a month and 3 minutes per conversation, and exporting transcripts requires Pro.

## Notta's pricing in 2026

- **Free:** 120 transcription minutes a month (no carryover), up to 3 minutes per conversation, 50 file uploads and 10 AI summaries a month. Exports are not included.
- **Pro:** $13.49/month, or $97.99/year ($8.17/month). 1,800 minutes a month, recordings up to 5 hours, transcript export and translation.
- **Business:** $27.99 per seat per month, or $199.99/year ($16.67/month). Unlimited transcription.

Notta is a capable meeting tool, with bot and bot-free recording, bilingual transcription and a mobile app. People mostly leave for three reasons: the free plan's 3-minute cap makes it a trial rather than a tool, exports sit behind Pro, and unused minutes don't roll over.

## 1. Fathom: best free meeting notetaker

Fathom's free plan is "free forever" with **unlimited recordings and transcription**, instant AI call summaries, and a choice of bot capture or bot-free recording (in beta on Mac). Premium costs **$16/month billed annually ($20 monthly)** and adds advanced summaries and action items, with a 90-day money-back guarantee. If Notta's 120-minute cap is the problem, this is the most direct fix.

## 2. Fireflies.ai: best for searchable team meetings

Fireflies' free plan advertises unlimited transcription for Zoom, Google Meet, Teams and more, but storage is capped at **400 minutes per team** and AI summaries are limited. Downloading transcripts starts on Pro at **$10/seat/month billed annually ($18 monthly)**. It suits teams who want every call searchable. See [YTTranscript vs Fireflies.ai](/yttranscript-vs-fireflies-ai).

## 3. Otter.ai: best for live transcription on the go

Otter's free Basic plan gives **300 minutes a month** with conversations up to 30 minutes, but only **3 lifetime file imports**, so it's built for recording rather than uploading. Pro is **$8.33/month billed annually ($16.99 monthly)** for 1,200 minutes and 90-minute meetings. More options in our [Otter alternatives](/otter-ai-alternatives) guide.

## 4. TurboScribe: best for bulk file uploads

TurboScribe transcribes **3 files a day free**, each up to 30 minutes. Its Unlimited plan costs **$10/month billed yearly ($20 monthly)** and accepts files up to 10 hours, 50 at a time, with translation into 134+ languages. For a backlog of recordings it beats Notta's per-minute quotas. See [TurboScribe alternatives](/turboscribe-alternatives) for the rest of that field.

## 5. YTTranscript: best for YouTube links

Notta can import YouTube links, but on the free plan it stops at 3 minutes and won't export. You get **2 free transcripts a day with no signup**, and one-time packs start at **$9 for 100 transcripts** with credits that never expire. For videos without captions, it can AI-generate a transcript (about 10 credits). The trade-off: it's YouTube-only, not a meeting recorder. ## 6. Whisper: best for privacy and offline use

OpenAI's open-source **Whisper** model runs on your own computer, so confidential audio never leaves it and there's no minute cap. The cost is setup time and hardware; the hosted API costs about $0.006 per minute. See [YTTranscript vs Whisper](/yttranscript-vs-whisper).

## The verdict

- **Free meeting notes without minute caps:** Fathom.
- **A whole team's calls, searchable:** Fireflies.ai.
- **Live transcription from your phone:** Otter.ai.
- **A pile of audio or video files:** TurboScribe.
- **YouTube videos:** [YTTranscript](https://yttranscript.app), with no account and no subscription.
- **Confidential recordings:** self-hosted Whisper.
- **Stay on Notta** if you rely on its bilingual transcription or bot-free recording and 1,800 Pro minutes a month is enough.
